Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Ari makes playable machines that show children how things work. This page explains what information we collect when you use learnwithari.co.uk, why we collect it, and what you can ask us to do with it.

The short version: children can play with everything on this site without giving us anything. The only personal information we collect is a parent's or carer's email address, and only if they choose to sign up. We never sell it.

Who we are

Ari is run by Khalifa Saber, a sole trader based in the UK, trading as Ari. For the purposes of UK data protection law, he is the "controller" of the information described here.

Contact: hello@learnwithari.co.uk

Children using the site

The machines on this site are made for children aged roughly 7 to 12. We designed the site so that children never need to tell us anything about themselves:

  • There are no accounts, logins or profiles.
  • Nothing a child does on a machine (pressing buttons, aiming panels, firing photons) is linked to who they are.
  • We don't use the microphone, camera or location. The engine sounds are made inside your browser.
  • There are no adverts and no chat.

The email sign-up is for parents and carers aged 18 or over. If you're under 18, please ask a grown-up to sign up for you. If we learn that a child has signed up, we'll delete their email address.

What we collect

1. If you sign up for emails. You give us your email address through the sign-up form. Along with it, the form records some details about your visit:

  • which machine page you signed up from (for example, "jet engine")
  • how much it was played before you signed up: a count of taps and how many of its challenges were completed
  • the link you arrived from, if it was tagged (for example, "instagram")

We use this to learn which machines and which places bring people to Ari. It isn't used to profile you, and it says nothing about who your child is.

2. Anonymous visit statistics. We use Vercel Web Analytics to count visits. It doesn't use cookies and doesn't store IP addresses. It records things like the page visited, the website you came from, your rough location (country and city), and your type of device and browser. It also records anonymous events, such as "the engine was lit" or "the plane took off", so we can see which parts of a machine work. Visits are grouped using a code generated from your request, which is thrown away after 24 hours. We only ever see totals.

3. Technical logs. Like any website, the servers that deliver this site (run by Vercel) keep short-lived technical logs, which can include IP addresses, to keep the site working and secure.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • Your email address: to send you the weekly Ari email, and news about Ari. Our legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by unsubscribing or emailing us.
  • Visit statistics and the sign-up details listed above: to understand which machines and which channels work, so we can make better ones. Our legal basis is our legitimate interests in running and improving the site. We've chosen tools that keep this anonymous or minimal.
  • Technical logs: to keep the site running and secure. Our legal basis is legitimate interests.

Who else handles it

We use a small number of service providers. They handle data on our behalf and may not use it for their own purposes.

  • Tally (based in Belgium) runs our sign-up form and stores sign-ups in Europe.
  • Vercel (based in the USA) hosts the site and provides the anonymous visit statistics.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces on our pages. When a page loads, your browser asks Google for the fonts, which means Google receives your IP address.

Where information goes outside the UK, we rely on the safeguards UK law requires, such as UK adequacy regulations or the standard contract clauses approved for international transfers.

If we start using a separate service to send the weekly email, we'll name it here first.

We never sell, rent or swap your information, and we never let anyone else use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • Your email address and its sign-up details: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them.
  • Visit statistics: kept as anonymous totals.
  • Technical logs: deleted automatically after a short period, set by our hosting provider.

Cookies

Our own pages don't set cookies, and our visit statistics work without them. The sign-up form is provided by Tally, and its privacy policy covers how the form itself works.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what information we hold about you, and give you a copy
  • correct it if it's wrong
  • delete it
  • stop using it, or limit how we use it
  • give it to you in a format you can take elsewhere

You can also withdraw your consent to emails at any time. Every email has an unsubscribe link. To do any of the above, email hello@learnwithari.co.uk. We'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we'll update this page and change the date at the top. If a change affects people on our email list, we'll tell you by email before it takes effect.
