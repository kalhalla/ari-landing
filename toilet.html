Fig. 02 · The toilet

Why does a toilet stop filling by itself?

Nobody turns it off. Press the handle and watch what does.

Water used0.0 L
1The handle lifts the flapper, a rubber plug at the bottom of the tank.
2Water rushes into the bowl. The float drops with it, and that opens the valve.
3Fresh water pours in. As the float rises, its arm pushes the valve shut.
!The float can't rise, so the valve never shuts. Extra water spills down the overflow pipe and runs away for ever. That's a "running toilet".
Your turn: what do you think happens if the float gets stuck at the bottom? Make a guess, then press Jam the float.

The big idea: a float is a switch the water flips by itself.
