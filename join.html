Questions parents ask

What age is it for?

Seven to eleven, which is Years 3 to 6. Children that age can read it and play it alone. A younger child can enjoy it with a grown-up alongside.

Is there an AI in it?

No. Your child never chats to an AI on Ari, and nothing they do is sent to one. Ari the owl's lines are written by us, mistakes included. Checking a confident answer is a habit, and this is a safe place to build it before they meet the real thing.

So how does it help with AI?

An AI is useful to someone who can tell when it is wrong. That takes two things: knowing how the subject really works, and being in the habit of checking. Ari practises both, ten times over. It does not teach your child to use an AI tool.

Does it replace school science?

No. Ari does not cover the whole curriculum. It gives your child a hands-on feel for the ideas, so the lesson makes sense when it comes.
