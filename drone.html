BREAK IT:

No wings, no rudder, just four propellers

A drone does everything by changing how fast each propeller spins.

01 · LIFT

Push air down

Each blade is a small tilted wing. As it swings round it throws air downwards, and the air pushes back up. Twice the speed gives four times the push.

02 · TILT

Lean to move

Speed up the back two propellers and the drone tips forward. Now some of the push points backwards, so it flies forwards.

03 · TURN

Twist to turn

Every spinning propeller twists the drone the opposite way. Two spin clockwise and two anticlockwise, so the twists cancel. Speed up one pair and the drone turns.

04 · BALANCE

A tiny pilot inside

A small computer called a flight controller checks the drone's angle hundreds of times a second and adjusts each motor to keep it level.

The big idea: pushing air down pushes the drone up. Everything else, from flying forwards to turning round, comes from making some propellers spin a bit faster than the others.
