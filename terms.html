What Ari is

Ari makes playable machines, such as a jet engine, a toilet cistern and a solar-powered house, that show children how things work. Everything on the site is free to play. You don't need an account.

For children, with a grown-up nearby

The machines are made for children aged roughly 7 to 12. We recommend a parent, carer or teacher is around, especially for younger children. Some machines make sounds. You can turn the sound off with the button on the machine.

And please don't take apart, light, or experiment on real engines, toilets, electrical equipment or solar panels because of something you tried here. The point of Ari is that you can break things safely on screen.
